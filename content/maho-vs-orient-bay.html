On a Philipsburg call, many guests choose between a Maho Beach runway stop and an Orient Beach French-side day. Both are excellent. They spend your hours differently.

Maho is iconic when aircraft are moving — but flight activity varies with airline schedules, weather and airport operations. Do not over-promise a specific landing time. Orient maximises swim, shade and lunch pacing.

Maho Beach vs Orient Beach

Maho Beach vs Orient Bay

Maho — planes & short visit

  • Runway-end beach west of Philipsburg when jets are active
  • Better as a focused stop than an all-day lounger plan
  • Stay behind barriers; jet blast on takeoff is dangerous
  • Treat aircraft sightings as possible, not scheduled entertainment

Orient — beach day

  • French-side clubs, turquoise water and longer sand time
  • Transfer times vary with traffic — often longer than Maho
  • Better when swimming and lunch matter more than runway photos
  • Confirm chair inclusions and return window with the operator

Mixing both on a long call

On a longer Philipsburg day, some guests do a shorter Maho stop and later Orient or a catamaran — two well-paced blocks beat three rushed attractions.

Check your ship’s all-aboard time and build your own buffer. Road times between west and east are variable, not exact.
