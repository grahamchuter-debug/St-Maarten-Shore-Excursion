Where ships arrive

Philipsburg terminals

Dr. A.C. Wathey Cruise & Cargo Facilities place you steps from Front Street duty-free shops, taxis and shore-excursion desks. Confirm your meeting point — multi-ship days get busy.

Getting to beaches

Maho Beach is typically about 15–25 minutes west by road — traffic varies. Orient Beach on the French side is often 25–45 minutes depending on congestion and your pickup point. Build your own return window.

Currency

Netherlands Antillean guilder (ANG) on Dutch side; euros in French St Martin. US dollars widely accepted.

Language

Dutch and English on Dutch side; French in Marigot. English common in tourism and at the port.

Two nations

Ordinary day movement between Dutch and French sides is typically open and low-friction — geography matters more than border theatre.
